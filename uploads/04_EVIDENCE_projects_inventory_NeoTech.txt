1. PEVE IA - AI-powered learning platform
Description:
PEVE IA is an educational platform designed to support students preparing for Chilean validation exams. It organizes learning by grade level, subject, and official learning objectives. It includes digital content, quizzes, simulations, downloadable materials, accessibility tools, AI tutoring, and dashboards for students, guardians, and educators.
Impact relevance:
- Supports educational continuity.
- Helps students outside traditional schooling.
- Provides structured and measurable learning pathways.
- Includes accessibility and inclusive design.

4. IoT and environmental monitoring
Description:
NEOTECH EDULAB has experience with sensors, ESP32/ESP8266 boards, data collection, automation, dashboards, and school-based prototypes. These systems can measure environmental variables such as temperature, humidity, water temperature, pressure, pH, and other indicators depending on the project.
Impact relevance:

- Supports climate and sustainability education.
