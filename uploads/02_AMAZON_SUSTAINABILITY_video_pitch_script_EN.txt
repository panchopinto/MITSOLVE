Amazon Sustainability Pilot Program - Elevator Pitch Script
Recommended duration: 90 to 120 seconds
Language: English

Hello, my name is Pancho Pinto, founder of NEOTECH EDULAB SpA, based in Chile.

Our solution for the Amazon Sustainability Pilot Program combines artificial intelligence, digital learning, accessibility tools, immersive educational resources, and environmental data systems to help students learn about sustainability through real-world experiences.

What makes our solution different is that we do not teach sustainability only as theory. We connect digital learning with practical school-based projects such as greenhouses, hydroponic systems, aquaponics, biodigesters, environmental sensors, and IoT-based monitoring.

Through our platform, students can access curriculum-aligned content, AI tutoring, quizzes, simulations, virtual and augmented reality resources, and guided learning activities. Teachers and families can also monitor progress and better support each student.

This is innovative because it brings together climate education, inclusive learning, and applied technology in one model. Students with different learning needs can access visual, interactive, and step-by-step materials, while schools can use real environmental data as part of the learning process.

NEOTECH EDULAB is well suited for the Amazon Sustainability Pilot Program because we already have experience developing educational platforms, inclusive content, immersive learning tools, IoT prototypes, and school-based innovation projects in Chile.

With Amazon's support, we want to validate, improve, and scale this model to more schools and communities, helping students become active problem-solvers for climate and sustainability challenges.

Thank you.
