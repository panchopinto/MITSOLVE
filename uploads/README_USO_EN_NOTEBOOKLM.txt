How to use these files in NotebookLM

1. Create a new NotebookLM notebook named:
   Amazon Sustainability Pitch - NEOTECH EDULAB

2. Upload all TXT files from this folder as sources.

3. Start with this prompt:
   Create a 90 to 120 second elevator pitch video structure for NEOTECH EDULAB SpA applying to the Amazon Sustainability Pilot Program. Use the uploaded sources. The video must explain the technology, what differentiates it, why it is innovative, and why NEOTECH EDULAB is well suited for the program.

4. Ask NotebookLM for:
   - Final English narration script.
   - Scene-by-scene storyboard.
   - Shot list.
   - On-screen text.
   - YouTube description.

5. Recommended final workflow:
   - Record Pancho speaking the full pitch.
   - Add B-roll images and screenshots in CapCut, Canva, Clipchamp, or InVideo.
   - Add English subtitles.
   - Export in 1080p.
   - Upload to YouTube as Unlisted.
   - Open the link in an incognito window to verify it works.
   - Send the link to Anna from MIT Solve.
